How it works

01Odds become goals

Bookmakers already price every match. We take their odds, remove their margin, and read off how many goals each team should score and concede.

02Goals become player points

Each team's goals are shared among its players by minutes, shots and chances created, then scored with FPL's own rules, bonus included.

03Points become your moves

The solver finds the best legal squad for each of the next ten weeks and shows the plan with the most points, plus a safer and a bolder option.
