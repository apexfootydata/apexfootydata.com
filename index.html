THE SOLVER

Your team, solved

Transfers, captain, bench order and chip timing over a 19-gameweek horizon — solved in your browser from your current squad, and re-solved the moment you change a player’s expected minutes. A fresh optimal 15 before the season; your live team once it’s underway.

THE ANCHOR

We don't argue with the market

Where the sharp books have priced a fixture, team goals are their de-vigged prices — we add no opinion of our own on top. But bookmakers only quote a week or two ahead, so the rest of the 19-gameweek horizon runs on our own xG model, and each fixture switches to the market the moment it is priced. The work is the conversion: prices into team goals, team goals into player points, player points into picks.

THE EVALUATION

Scored in public

Every projection is frozen before the deadline and hashed, so it cannot be quietly edited afterwards. As each gameweek settles it is scored against what actually happened — hits and misses alike — on the Model Evaluation page. The log starts at GW1 and fills in weekly.
