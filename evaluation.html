How it is scored

Frozen before the deadline. Every tool’s projection is saved before the gameweek starts and never changed after.
Same players for all three. Players owned by 5% or more of managers at the deadline, matched by FPL player ID: 352 so far.
Lower is closer. The number is how many points a projection missed by, on average, per player. A blank or a benching counts as whatever it scored.
Only totals are shown. The paid tools are named Tool A and Tool B, and their individual projections are never published.
4 player-gameweeks were set aside because at least one tool published no projection for that player.
