Where these numbers come from

Where bookmakers have priced a match, FPL Apex takes their odds, removes the bookmaker's margin, and turns them into goals, assists and clean sheets for every player. Matches further ahead use team ratings built from past market prices. It combines those with each player's expected minutes to give FPL points. Every projection is saved before the deadline and scored afterwards on the Model scorecard. Read how it works.
