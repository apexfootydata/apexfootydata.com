Where these numbers come from

Bookmakers price every match. FPL Apex takes those odds, removes the bookmaker's margin, and turns them into goals, assists, clean sheets and minutes for every player, then into FPL points. Every projection is saved before the deadline and scored afterwards on the Model scorecard. Read how it works.
